The listening room

Eight synthesized performances, before and after the delay. Switch between dry and processed at the same playback position.

These recordings were rendered from the software emulation, not a physical pedal. Each pair uses the same performance and source gain. Two pattern cycles are followed by ten seconds of decay. Headphones reveal the stereo movement.

48 kHz · stereo · 16-bit PCM WAV · no independent normalization
